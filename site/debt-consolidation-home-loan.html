Debt consolidation home loans

Rolling car loans, personal loans and credit cards into your home loan can cut monthly repayments. Done carelessly, it can also cost more over time.

How it works and the trade-offs

One lower repayment

Home loan rates are usually lower than credit card and personal loan rates, so combining debts often reduces your monthly outgoings.

Longer loan term

Spreading a short-term debt over a long mortgage term can mean paying more interest overall, unless you keep making higher repayments.

Equity required

You need enough equity in your home to absorb the extra debt, usually within a lender's limits.

Close what you clear

Consolidation works best when you close cleared cards and accounts so the debt doesn't return.
