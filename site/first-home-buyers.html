Your first home, without the guesswork

Buying for the first time means a lot of new terms and a lot of decisions. We explain each step before you make an offer, so there are no surprises at settlement.

What we can help with

Deposit and savings

We'll show how different deposit sizes change your repayments and whether Lenders Mortgage Insurance applies, so you can decide whether to buy sooner or save for longer.

Grants and schemes

Government grants, stamp duty concessions and guarantee schemes differ by state and change over time. We check what you may be eligible for at the time you buy.

The costs beyond the price

Stamp duty, legal fees, inspections and moving costs add up. We'll help you build a realistic budget for the whole purchase, not just the deposit.

Pre-approval before you search

Knowing your likely borrowing range before you inspect homes helps you bid with confidence and avoid falling for a property you can't finance.

Talk it through

A short call is usually enough to see what's realistic. No cost and no obligation.
