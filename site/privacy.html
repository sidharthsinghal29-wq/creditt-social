Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Who we are

Creditt Pty Ltd (ABN 43 609 771 226) is a credit representative (CRN 533758). We also provide accounting and tax services through our registered tax agents. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Creditt. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

We collect what you give us and what we need to help you. Depending on the service, this can include:

  • Contact details: name, phone, email and address.
  • Identity details: date of birth and, when we start work, identification documents.
  • Financial details: income, expenses, assets, debts, loan statements and credit history.
  • Tax details: income, deductions, investments, rental property and vehicle information.
  • Website data: pages visited, device and browser type, and how you arrived, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

Our online forms do not ask for your tax file number or bank account details. We collect those separately, and only when we need them.

Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and call you back.
  • Assess your needs and recommend suitable loan or finance options.
  • Prepare loan applications and submit them to lenders.
  • Prepare and lodge tax returns and provide accounting services.
  • Meet legal obligations, including responsible lending, anti-money laundering and tax agent requirements.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Tell you about services that may suit you. You can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We may share your information with:

  • Lenders, lenders' mortgage insurers, valuers and other parties involved in your application.
  • Our licensee and aggregators or other service providers who support our broking business.
  • Credit reporting bodies, where you have authorised a credit check.
  • The Australian Taxation Office and other government bodies, where you have authorised us to act for you or the law requires it.
  • Our professional advisers, IT and website providers, and email and analytics providers.
  • Anyone else you have asked us to share it with, or the law requires or permits.

Some of our providers may store data outside Australia. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is protected.

Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and tools such as Google Analytics and advertising tags to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, including access controls and secure systems. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not email sensitive details such as your tax file number or bank account numbers. Call us and we will arrange a secure way to send them.

We keep records for as long as the law requires, then securely destroy or de-identify them.

Access and corrections

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity first.

Complaints

If you think we have breached your privacy, tell us in writing at hello@creditt.com.au. We will investigate and reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992. For credit services, you can also contact the Australian Financial Complaints Authority at afca.org.au or 1800 931 678.
