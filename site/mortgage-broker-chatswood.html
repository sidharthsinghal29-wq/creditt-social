Mortgage broker in Chatswood

Chatswood is one of the busiest lending markets on the North Shore. We help apartment buyers, investors, families and business owners compare lenders and structure loans that suit.

What we see in Chatswood

High-rise apartments near the interchange

Chatswood has a large stock of apartments close to the train and Metro. Lenders look closely at unit size, building density and the developer, so the right lender for one apartment can be the wrong one for the next.

Investors

Many Chatswood purchases are investment properties. Structure matters: interest-only periods, splitting loans for deductibility, and keeping your home loan separate. See investment loans.

Income from overseas or multiple sources

Some buyers have income, assets or family support from overseas, or income from a mix of salary and business. Lenders vary widely in how they assess this, so comparing policies matters as much as comparing rates.

Heritage homes in Chatswood West

Conservation areas and older homes can affect renovation plans and valuations. Talk to us early if you're planning works.
