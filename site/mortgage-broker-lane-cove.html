What we see in Lane Cove

Apartments around the village and Lane Cove West

Much of the newer stock is apartments, including off-the-plan buildings. Lenders treat these differently: valuations happen at completion, and some lenders cap lending on small units or large high-density complexes. We check the building against lender policy before you commit.

Family homes that are tightly held

Houses in Lane Cove don't come up often, so many upgraders find their next home before they've sold. That's where pre-approval that counts your existing equity, or a bridging loan, can make the difference.

Waterfront and prestige pockets

Riverview, Longueville and Northwood include higher-value homes where loan size, lender appetite and valuation risk all matter more. Not every lender treats large loans the same way.

Local business owners

The Lane Cove West business park means plenty of self-employed clients. If your income comes through a company or trust, see self-employed home loans.
