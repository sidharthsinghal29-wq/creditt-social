Mortgage broker in Mosman and Neutral Bay

Mosman and Neutral Bay buyers often deal with larger loans, upgrades and more complex finances. We compare lenders and structure finance for prestige and family purchases alike.

What we see in Mosman and Neutral Bay

Larger loan sizes

Many purchases here involve loans well above the Sydney average. Lenders set different limits and conditions for large loans, and pricing can be negotiated. Getting several lenders' views matters more at this end of the market.

Upgrading without selling first

Families moving from an apartment to a house often need to buy before selling. We work through equity release and bridging finance so you can bid with confidence.

Apartments in Neutral Bay and Cremorne

Older blocks and harbourside apartments sit alongside new developments. Strata, building age and size all affect lender appetite.

Complex income

Business owners, partners in firms and people with trust or investment income need lenders who understand their structures. See self-employed home loans.
