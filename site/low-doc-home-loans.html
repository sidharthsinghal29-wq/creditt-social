Low doc home loans

If your tax returns aren't up to date or don't reflect what you earn now, a low doc loan may let you verify income another way.

How low doc loans work

Alternative income evidence

Instead of full tax returns, lenders may accept recent BAS statements, business bank statements or a declaration from your accountant.

Larger deposit, higher cost

Low doc loans usually need a bigger deposit and can carry higher rates or fees than standard loans, because the lender takes on more risk.

ABN history

Most lenders want your ABN and GST registration to have been active for a minimum period, often one to two years.

A stepping stone

Many borrowers use a low doc loan for a few years, then refinance to a standard loan once returns are up to date. See refinancing.
