Refinancing checklist: steps and documents

Thinking of refinancing? Work through this checklist first so you know whether it's worth it and what to have ready.

Step 1: Know your current loan

  • Current balance, rate and repayment.
  • Whether any part is fixed, and the break cost if so.
  • Discharge fees and features you use, such as an offset or redraw.

Step 2: Ask your current lender first

A quick call asking for a rate review sometimes gets a better rate without switching. Use it as a benchmark.

Step 3: Compare the total cost

Add up exit costs and new loan costs, then work out how long it takes for the lower repayment to pay them back. Our guide five questions to ask before you refinance walks through it.

Step 4: Gather your documents

  • Photo ID.
  • Recent payslips, or tax returns if self-employed.
  • Recent statements for your home loan and any other debts.
  • Council rates notice and home insurance details.

Step 5: Apply, value and settle

The new lender values your property, approves the loan and arranges settlement with your current lender. It typically takes two to six weeks.

Step 6: After you switch

Set up repayments, move your offset savings, and keep your repayments at the old amount if you can afford it, to pay the loan down faster.
