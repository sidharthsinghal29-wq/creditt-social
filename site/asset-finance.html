Asset finance that keeps your cash working

Buy the vehicles, machinery and equipment your business needs without tying up your cash. We compare lenders and structures to match the asset and your cash flow.

What we can help with

Chattel mortgage

You own the asset from day one and the lender holds security over it. Common for vehicles and equipment, with an optional final balloon payment to lower repayments.

Finance lease

The lender owns the asset and you pay to use it for an agreed term. At the end you may return it, extend or buy it, depending on the agreement.

Equipment rental

Flexible use of equipment that dates quickly, such as technology. Terms and tax treatment vary, so ask your accountant.

What we can finance

Vehicles, trucks, machinery, medical and hospitality equipment and more, depending on the lender and the asset's age and type.
