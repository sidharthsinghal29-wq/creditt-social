Commercial finance, structured around how your business trades

Commercial property, business loans and equipment finance work differently to a home loan. The right structure depends on your industry, your cash flow and what the asset is for.

What we can help with

Commercial property

Buying the premises your business operates from, or a commercial property as an investment, generally involves lower maximum LVRs and shorter terms than residential lending. We compare bank and non-bank lenders who specialise in your property type.

Business loans and working capital

A term loan or line of credit can fund growth, stock or a cash flow gap. Lenders often look at trading history and cash flow more than personal income, and decisions can be faster than property lending.

Equipment and asset finance

Vehicles, machinery and specialist equipment can usually be financed through a chattel mortgage, finance lease or rental agreement. The right structure depends on how you want to treat the asset for tax. Your accountant can advise.

Bridging and short-term finance

If timing doesn't line up, such as settling on a new property before an existing one sells, short-term finance can cover the gap, usually at a higher rate that reflects the shorter term.
