First home buyer guide for NSW (2026)

Buying your first home in Sydney is expensive, but government help can make a real difference to how much you need upfront. Here's what's available in NSW in 2026, and how to make it work.

1. Stamp duty: the First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme

Eligible first home buyers in NSW pay no transfer (stamp) duty on new or existing homes up to $800,000, and a reduced amount on homes between $800,000 and $1,000,000. For vacant land you intend to build on, the full exemption applies up to $350,000, with a concession up to $450,000. Above those caps, full duty applies. Eligibility rules, including living in the home, apply, so check the details with Revenue NSW.

2. The 5% Deposit Scheme

The Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme (formerly the Home Guarantee Scheme) lets eligible first home buyers buy with a 5% deposit without paying lenders mortgage insurance. The government guarantees up to 15% of the property value to the lender. Since October 2025 there are no income caps and no limit on places, and the price cap for Sydney is $1.5 million. You apply through a participating lender, which a broker can arrange.

3. The First Home Owner Grant (new homes)

A $10,000 grant may be available for eligible buyers of a new home, including off-the-plan, valued up to $600,000, or for building a new home within the relevant cap. It doesn't apply to existing homes.

4. Your deposit and the real upfront costs

  • Deposit: from 5% with the scheme, 20% to avoid LMI without it, or somewhere in between with LMI.
  • Stamp duty: possibly nil or reduced, as above.
  • Conveyancing, building and pest inspections, and loan fees.
  • A buffer for moving costs and the first few months.

5. How much can you borrow?

Lenders look at your income, expenses, existing debts and deposit, and test repayments at a rate higher than the actual rate. See how much can I borrow and try our borrowing power calculator.

6. Get pre-approval before you bid

Pre-approval tells you your real limit, so you can bid at auction or make offers with confidence. We'll also check which schemes you qualify for and line up a participating lender.
