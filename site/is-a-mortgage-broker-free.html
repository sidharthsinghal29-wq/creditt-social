Is a mortgage broker free? How brokers are paid

For most home loans, using a mortgage broker costs you nothing directly. Here's how it works and what protects you.

How brokers are paid

When your loan settles, the lender usually pays the broker an upfront commission, and often a smaller ongoing 'trail' commission while the loan is active. These payments come from the lender, not from your loan amount.

When a fee might apply

Some brokers charge a fee for complex or commercial lending, or if a loan is repaid very early. Any fee should be explained to you in writing before you proceed.

The best interests duty

Mortgage brokers in Australia must act in your best interests when giving credit assistance, and put your interests ahead of their own. That includes how they choose lenders to recommend.

What your broker must tell you

Your broker gives you a credit guide explaining who they are, how they're paid, and the lenders on their panel. At Creditt, we can only recommend products from lenders we have access to, and we explain why each option is on your shortlist.

Questions worth asking any broker

  • How many lenders do you compare?
  • Will you charge me any fee?
  • Why are you recommending this lender over others?
