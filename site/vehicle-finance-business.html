Business vehicle finance, from a single ute to a fleet

Utes, vans, trucks and fleets can be financed in your business name or as a sole trader. We compare lenders so the repayments suit the work the vehicle does.

What we can help with

Utes and vans for tradies

Many lenders finance work vehicles for sole traders and small businesses. We'll check which suit your trading history.

Sole traders and ABN holders

Lenders look at how long you've held your ABN and your income evidence. We'll tell you what to prepare.

Trucks and heavy vehicles

Heavier vehicles often have different lender rules on age and use. We'll match you with lenders who finance them.

Fleets and multiple vehicles

One facility for several vehicles can simplify paperwork. We'll compare it with separate loans.
