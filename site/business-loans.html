Business loans and lines of credit

Fund growth, stock or a cash flow gap with finance that suits how your business trades. We compare lenders and explain the trade-offs.

What we can help with

Term loans

A fixed amount repaid over an agreed term, useful for one-off costs like an expansion.

Lines of credit

A limit you can draw on and repay as needed, useful for managing cash flow.

Limited documentation options

Some lenders offer options for newer or self-employed businesses with limited paperwork. These often cost more, and we'll explain why.

Secured or unsecured

Security can lower the cost, but it puts an asset on the line. We'll explain the trade-off for your situation.

Talk it through
