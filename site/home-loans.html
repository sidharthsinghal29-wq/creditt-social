Home loans, matched to how you'll pay them off

Whether you want the certainty of a fixed rate or the flexibility of an offset account, we shortlist the structures that fit your income and your timeline, not just the lowest advertised rate.

What we can help with

Fixed rate

Your repayment stays the same for a set period, usually one to five years. Useful if you want certainty while a household budget settles in, though most fixed loans limit extra repayments and charge a break fee if you exit early.

Variable rate

Repayments move with the lender's rate, but you typically get unlimited extra repayments, redraw and often an offset account. Those features can meaningfully reduce the interest you pay over time.

Split loan

Part fixed, part variable. A middle ground for borrowers who want some payment certainty without giving up all the flexibility of a variable loan.

Offset account

A transaction account linked to your loan. Every dollar sitting in it reduces the interest calculated on your loan balance, without locking the money away.
