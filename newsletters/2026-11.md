# The Creditt Report: November 2026 (published edition summary)
Headline: Rates up, prices down: what it means for your home loan
Subtitle: Five rate rises, seven months of falling values, and why this could still be a good time to act.
Sections: 1) Rates: RBA lifted cash rate to 4.85% on 3 Nov (5th rise in 2026, highest since 2008; 3 of 9 voted to hold; statement dropped warning of further rises). 2) Property: Cotality Oct national -1.2%, 7th monthly fall, ~6.7% below March peak; Sydney -1.6% in Oct, 10%+ below Feb peak. 3) First home buyers: 5% Deposit Scheme (no LMI, no income caps, Sydney cap $1.5m), NSW stamp duty exemption to $800k.
Checklist: rate review, fixed expiry check, update pre-approval, Christmas credit card limits, talk to lender early if repayments tight.
Dates to watch: early December RBA decision and Cotality index; holiday lender slowdown late Dec to mid Jan.
