Can your parents help you buy without handing over cash?

A family guarantee lets a parent use equity in their own home as extra security for your loan, which can mean a smaller deposit and no lenders mortgage insurance. It also carries real risk for the guarantor.

Our new guide explains how it works, limited vs full guarantees, the risks for parents, and how to get the guarantee released later.
