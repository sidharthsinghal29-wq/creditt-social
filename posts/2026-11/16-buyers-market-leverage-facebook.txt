If you've been waiting for the market to give buyers a bit more breathing room, it's happening. 🏡

Cotality's latest figures show national home values fell 1.2% in October, the seventh monthly fall in a row, and are now 6.7% below the March peak. Homes are taking a median 39 days to sell, and the average vendor discount is 4.7%, the biggest since May 2019. Brisbane, Perth and Sydney saw the largest falls last month.

That means more choice and more room to negotiate. The buyers who make the most of it are the ones with their finance ready to go:

• Get pre-approved before you start making offers
• Work out repayments you're comfortable with at today's rates (the RBA cash rate is now 4.85%)
• Leave room for stamp duty, legals and moving costs. These vary by state, so check the rules where you're buying
• Already have a loan? It's a good time for a quick health check
