Cotality's latest Home Value Index shows national home values fell 1.2% in October, the seventh monthly fall in a row, leaving values 6.7% below the March peak. Homes are taking a median 39 days to sell and the average vendor discount has widened to 4.7%, the largest since May 2019. Brisbane, Perth and Sydney recorded the biggest monthly falls.

With the RBA cash rate now at 4.85%, the advantage goes to buyers whose finance is sorted before they start negotiating:

• Get pre-approved so you can move quickly and negotiate with confidence
• Set your budget on repayments that still work at today's rates, not the maximum a lender might offer
• Keep a buffer for stamp duty, legals and moving costs (these vary by state, so check yours)
• Already own? A softer market is a good time to check your loan is still competitive
