Refinancing? Check your offset actually works 👀

Refinances are now about one in three home loan deals (Quickli, Nov 2026), and Australians held about $349b in offset accounts in March 2026 (ASIC). ASIC found some offsets were opened but never linked, so the savings didn't happen.

Quick checks:
• Offset linked to the right loan?
• Re-check after any refinance or product switch
• Can you see the interest saving on statements?
• Weigh offset fees against using redraw

Offset and redraw can both cut the interest you pay. The right one depends on how you use your savings.
