Refinancing? Check your offset account works.

Refinances now make up about one in three home loan deals (Quickli, November 2026), and Australians held about $349 billion in mortgage offset accounts in March 2026 (ASIC). ASIC found some offset accounts were opened but not linked, or linked late, so borrowers missed out on interest savings.

Quick checks:
- Confirm your offset is linked to the right loan
- Re-check the link after any refinance or product switch
- Look for the interest saving on your statements
- Weigh any package fee or rate difference against using redraw
