Offset or redraw? It's one of the most common questions I get, and there's no one right answer.

Both can reduce the interest you pay on your home loan. The difference is how you use your money:

• An offset account keeps your savings in an everyday account linked to your loan
• Redraw lets you take back extra repayments you've already made
• Offset packages can come with fees or a different rate, so it's worth checking whether the saving outweighs the cost
• If you refinance or switch products, confirm your offset is still linked. ASIC has found cases where offsets weren't linked, so borrowers missed savings
