For the first time in a while, buyers across Australia have real choice.

The catch is that you need your finance sorted to take advantage of it. What I'm suggesting to clients right now:
• Get pre-approved before you make an offer
• Set a budget around repayments you're comfortable with, not the maximum you could borrow
• Allow for stamp duty and purchase costs, which vary by state
• If you already own, book a loan health check
