Running a business and paying off a home? This month's rate rise probably landed twice.

On 3 November the RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85%, the fifth increase this year. Around half of small business credit in Australia is secured against residential property, so for lots of business owners, business debt and the family home are closely tied together.

The good news: the RBA says business credit is still readily available for most borrowers. So if your finance hasn't had a look in a while, here are a few simple checks:

• List all your facilities (business loans, overdrafts, equipment and vehicle finance) and what each one is secured against
• Put a cash flow buffer like a line of credit in place before the quiet months, not during them
• Where it suits, use finance secured by the equipment or vehicle itself, rather than the house
• Check your pricing and loan structure still stack up
