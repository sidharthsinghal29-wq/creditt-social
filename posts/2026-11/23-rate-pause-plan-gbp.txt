A rate pause isn't a rate cut. The RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85% in November, its fifth rise of 2026, on a 6–3 Board vote. CommBank expects a hold at the 7–8 December meeting, and the RBA forecasts inflation above 3% until the end of 2027. Meanwhile the combined capitals auction clearance rate was 41.3% in the week to 15 November (Cotality), down from 63.1% a year ago.

What borrowers can do now: budget on rates staying near today's level for a while, build a buffer in your offset or redraw, and check whether your loan is still competitive, as lenders' offers and policies differ. If you're buying, a quieter market can give finance-ready buyers more room to negotiate.
