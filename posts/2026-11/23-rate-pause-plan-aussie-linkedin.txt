"Rates are about to come down, right?" I'm hearing this a lot, and I'd be careful planning around it.

The RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85% in November, its fifth rise this year. A hold is now widely tipped for the 7–8 December meeting, but the RBA itself expects inflation to stay above 3% until the end of 2027. A pause is not the same as a cut.

So rather than waiting for relief, here's what I'd suggest for homeowners right across Australia:

• Check your rate against what lenders are offering new customers
• Budget your repayments at today's rate, not a hoped-for cut
• Build a buffer in your offset account if you have one
• If your fixed rate ends in the next few months, start planning the switch early
