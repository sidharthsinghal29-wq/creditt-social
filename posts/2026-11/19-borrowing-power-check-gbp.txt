Has your borrowing power changed this year? For many Australians, the answer is yes.

The RBA has lifted the cash rate five times in 2026, by 1.25 percentage points to 4.85%. Lenders must assess new borrowers at least 3 percentage points above the loan rate, so higher rates can reduce how much you can be approved for. ABS data shows the number of new home loans fell 15.0% in the September quarter.

Planning to buy or refinance? A few practical steps:
- Get any pre-approval from earlier in the year re-checked before you bid
- Reduce unused credit card limits, as lenders usually assess the limit
- Tidy up smaller debts such as car loans and buy now, pay later
- Remember lender policies differ, so your result can vary between lenders
