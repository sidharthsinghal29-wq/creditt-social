A rate pause would be welcome. But a pause isn't a cut.

The RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85% in November, its fifth rise this year. The minutes show the Board split 6–3, with three members preferring to hold. CommBank now expects the cash rate to stay at 4.85% at the 7–8 December meeting, and the RBA's own forecasts have inflation above 3% until the end of 2027.

The property market is feeling it. Cotality's combined capitals auction clearance rate was 41.3% in the week to 15 November, down from 63.1% a year earlier and lower than a year ago in every capital.

What we're suggesting to borrowers across Australia:

• Budget on rates staying around today's level for a while, rather than counting on relief
• Build a buffer in your offset or redraw while you can
• Ask whether your current loan is still competitive. Lenders' offers and policies differ
• Buying? A quieter market can give finance-ready buyers more room to negotiate
