Five rate rises in 2026. Is your business debt set up the right way?

Quick finance check-up:
• List every facility and what it's secured against
• Set up a cash flow buffer before you need it
• Match finance to the asset (equipment, vehicles) where it suits
• Check your pricing and structure still stack up
