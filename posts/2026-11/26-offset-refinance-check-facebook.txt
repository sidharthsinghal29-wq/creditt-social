Is your offset account actually saving you money? 🤔

More Australians are refinancing right now. Broker data from Quickli shows refinances are about one in three home loan deals, up from about one in four a year ago. And Aussies had around $349 billion sitting in offset accounts in March 2026 (ASIC).

Here's the catch: ASIC found some offset accounts were opened but never linked to the home loan, or linked late. That means the savings people expected didn't happen.

So, a quick to-do list for this weekend:

✅ Check your offset is linked to the right loan (your app or statement will show it)
✅ If you've refinanced or switched products, check the link again
✅ Look for the interest saving on your statements, and ask your lender if it's not there
✅ Compare any offset package fee or rate difference with simply using redraw

Offset and redraw can both reduce the interest you pay. The better fit comes down to how you use your savings day to day.
