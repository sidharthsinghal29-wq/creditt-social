Five rate rises in 2026. Is your business debt still set up the right way?

The RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85% on 3 November, its fifth increase this year. For many business owners that hits twice: once through business loans and again through the home loan. Around half of small business credit in Australia is secured against residential property, so the line between business and household finances is often thin.

The RBA also notes business credit growth remains strong, so lenders are still keen to lend to well-prepared businesses. That makes now a good time for a finance check-up:

• Map every facility: business loans, overdrafts, equipment and vehicle finance, and what each is secured against
• Set up a cash flow buffer, such as a line of credit or overdraft, before you need it, not in a crunch
• Match the finance to the asset: equipment or vehicle finance secured by the asset can help keep the family home out of it
• Ask whether your current pricing and structure are still competitive
