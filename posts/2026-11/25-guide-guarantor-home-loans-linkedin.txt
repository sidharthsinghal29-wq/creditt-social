New guide: guarantor home loans and how a family guarantee works.

A family guarantee lets a parent (or another close relative) use equity in their own home as extra security for your loan. It can mean buying with a small deposit and avoiding lenders mortgage insurance. But the guarantor takes on real risk, and that part deserves just as much attention.

The guide covers:
• How the guarantee fills the gap to a 20% deposit
• Limited vs full guarantees, and why the difference matters
• The risks for parents, including their own borrowing and credit file
• How and when to get the guarantee released
• When the 5% Deposit Scheme might suit a Sydney buyer better
