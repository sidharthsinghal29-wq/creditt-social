Investors have stepped back sharply. New investor home loan commitments fell 30.6% in the September quarter, the ABS reported this month, while the value of investor lending fell for a third straight quarter.

If you're still weighing up an investment property, a quieter market can be a chance to plan properly rather than rush. Before making an offer, I'd check:

• Your repayments at today's rates, with a buffer for further changes
• How the lender assesses rental income, as many only count part of it
• Interest-only vs principal and interest, and what happens to repayments when an interest-only period ends
• A cash cushion for vacancies, repairs and rate rises

Lender policies differ, so the same numbers can look quite different from one lender to the next.
