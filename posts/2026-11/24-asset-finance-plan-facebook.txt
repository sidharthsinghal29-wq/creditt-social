Has your business been holding off on new equipment or a new work vehicle this year? You're in good company.

Equifax's latest Business Market Pulse (September 2026) shows small and medium business loan enquiries up 14% on a year ago, while asset finance applications were down 3.2%. Lots of business owners have been making older gear last longer and lining up cash flow facilities instead. The good news: Equifax says the asset finance "freeze looks to be thawing".

If an upgrade is coming up in 2027, here's what we'd suggest thinking about:

• Use asset finance for equipment and vehicles where it suits, so your cash stays free for wages, stock and suppliers
• Match the loan term to how long the asset will actually work for you
• Compare chattel mortgage, lease and hire purchase on monthly cash flow, ownership and what happens at the end
• Get your recent financials and supplier quotes together early, as a complete application helps things move faster

With the cash rate at 4.85%, getting the structure right matters just as much as the price.
