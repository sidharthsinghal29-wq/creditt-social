Planning an equipment or vehicle upgrade in 2027? Plan the finance first. Equifax's September 2026 Business Market Pulse shows small and medium business loan enquiries up 14% year on year, while asset finance applications were down 3.2%, an easing from a 12.7% fall in August. Many businesses have been making older equipment last longer and securing cash lines instead.

Finance basics worth checking before you buy: use asset finance for equipment and vehicles where it suits, so working capital stays free for wages, stock and suppliers; match the loan term to the asset's working life; compare chattel mortgage, lease and hire purchase on cash flow, ownership and end-of-term options; and have recent financials and supplier quotes ready. With the cash rate at 4.85%, structure matters as much as price.
