Saving a 20% deposit for a Sydney home can take years. For many first home buyers, it doesn't have to.

Under the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme, eligible first home buyers can buy with a 5% deposit and avoid lenders mortgage insurance. There are no income caps and no waiting list, and the price cap for Sydney is $1.5 million.

The market backdrop has shifted too. Cotality reports Sydney dwelling values are more than 10% below their February peak, while five cash rate rises this year have reduced how much many households can borrow.

Before you start inspecting:
• Get pre-approval first, so you know your real budget at today's rates
• Check your target suburbs and price range sit under the scheme cap
• Budget for stamp duty, legal and moving costs on top of your deposit
• Stress-test repayments, as a smaller deposit means a bigger loan
