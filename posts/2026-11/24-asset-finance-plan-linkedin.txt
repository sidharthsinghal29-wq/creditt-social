Putting off that equipment or vehicle upgrade? Plan the finance before you need it.

Equifax's September 2026 Business Market Pulse shows small and medium business loan enquiries up 14% on a year earlier, while asset finance applications were down 3.2%. Many businesses have been making older equipment last longer and securing cash lines instead. Equifax says the asset finance "freeze looks to be thawing", after a 12.7% fall in August.

If an upgrade is on your 2027 list, a few finance basics help:

• Fund equipment and vehicles with asset finance where it suits, so working capital stays free for wages, stock and suppliers
• Match the loan term to the asset's working life
• Compare chattel mortgage, lease and hire purchase on monthly cash flow, ownership and end-of-term options
• Have recent financials and supplier quotes ready, as a complete application helps lenders move faster

With the cash rate at 4.85% after the RBA's November rise, the structure matters as much as the price.
