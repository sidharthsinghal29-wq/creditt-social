Got a pre-approval from earlier this year? It might be worth a second look.

The RBA has raised the cash rate five times in 2026, up 1.25 percentage points to 4.85%. Because lenders test new borrowers at least 3 percentage points above the loan rate, each rise can trim how much you're able to borrow, even if your income hasn't changed.

Plenty of buyers are pausing. ABS figures show the number of new home loans across Australia fell 15.0% in the September quarter.

If you're still keen to buy (or refinance), here are a few simple things that can help:

👉 Have your borrowing power re-checked before you make an offer
👉 Lower or close credit cards you don't use. Lenders usually count the full limit
👉 Pay down or tidy up smaller debts like car loans and buy now, pay later
👉 Don't assume one lender's answer is the only answer. Policies differ
