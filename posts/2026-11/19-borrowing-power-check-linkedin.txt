Five rate rises this year means many buyers can borrow less than they could in January.

The RBA has lifted the cash rate by 1.25 percentage points in 2026, to 4.85%. Lenders must test new borrowers at least 3 percentage points above the loan rate (APRA's serviceability buffer), so every rise flows straight into what you can be approved for.

Buyers have noticed. ABS data shows the number of new home loans fell 15.0% in the September quarter, with the value of new lending recording its largest quarterly fall since the series began in 2002.

If you're planning to buy or refinance, a few practical steps:

• Pre-approval from earlier in the year? Get it re-checked before you bid
• Cut unused credit card limits. Lenders usually assess the limit, not the balance
• Tidy up smaller debts like car loans and buy now, pay later accounts
• Remember lender policies differ, so how you're assessed can vary
