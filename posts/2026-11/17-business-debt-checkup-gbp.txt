Five rate rises in 2026: time for a business finance check-up. The RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85% on 3 November, its fifth increase this year. Around half of small business credit in Australia is secured against residential property, so many owners feel rate rises in both their business and their home loan. Simple checks worth doing now: list every facility (business loans, overdrafts, equipment and vehicle finance) and what it is secured against; set up a cash flow buffer such as a line of credit before you need it; where it suits, use finance secured by the asset itself rather than the family home; and make sure your pricing and structure are still competitive.
