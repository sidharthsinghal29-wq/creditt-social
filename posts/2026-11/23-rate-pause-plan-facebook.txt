Hoping for a rate cut soon? Here's where things stand.

The RBA raised the cash rate to 4.85% this month, the fifth rise of 2026, and the Board was split 6–3 on the decision. CommBank expects rates to stay on hold at the RBA's 7–8 December meeting, and the RBA expects inflation to stay above 3% until the end of 2027. So a pause may be on the cards, but relief is unlikely to come quickly.

Buyers are cautious too. Only 41.3% of capital city auctions cleared in the week to 15 November (Cotality), compared with 63.1% a year ago.

A few practical things you can do now, wherever you live in Australia:

• Plan your budget around today's repayments
• Put spare cash into your offset or redraw as a buffer
• Check your loan is still competitive (lenders differ a lot)
• If you're buying, being finance-ready can help you negotiate in a quieter market
