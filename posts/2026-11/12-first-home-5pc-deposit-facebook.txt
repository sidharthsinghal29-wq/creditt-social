Dreaming of your first home but feel like the deposit is always out of reach? 🏡

Here's some good news: under the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme, eligible first home buyers can buy with just a 5% deposit and no lenders mortgage insurance. There are no income caps, no waiting list, and the price cap in Sydney is $1.5 million.

Sydney values have also eased, sitting more than 10% below their February peak (Cotality). The flip side is that five rate rises this year have trimmed how much many people can borrow, so planning matters more than ever.

A few things to do before you start going to open homes:
• Get pre-approved so you know your real budget
• Make sure the homes you like sit under the price cap
• Put money aside for stamp duty, legals and moving costs
• Work out repayments you're comfortable with on a bigger loan
