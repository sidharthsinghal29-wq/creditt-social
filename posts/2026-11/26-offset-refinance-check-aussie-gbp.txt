Offset or redraw? Pick what suits you.

Both can reduce the interest you pay on your home loan. An offset account keeps your savings in an everyday account linked to your loan. Redraw lets you take back extra repayments you've made. Offset packages can carry fees or a different rate, so check the saving outweighs the cost. Refinancing or switching products? Confirm your offset is still linked.
