Refinancing is now about one in three home loan deals. Before you switch, check your offset account is actually doing its job.

Recent broker data from Quickli shows refinances make up roughly one in three deals, up from about one in four a year ago. At the same time, Australians held about $349 billion in mortgage offset accounts as of March 2026 (ASIC). ASIC's review found some offset accounts were opened but never linked, or linked late, so borrowers missed out on interest savings.

A few practical checks, whether you're refinancing or staying put:

• Confirm your offset is linked to the right loan, using your app or statements
• Re-check the link after any refinance, product switch or loan split
• Look for the interest saving on your statements and ask your lender if you can't see it
• Weigh any package fee or rate difference against using redraw instead

Offset and redraw can both reduce the interest you pay. Which suits you depends on how you use your savings and what your loan costs.
