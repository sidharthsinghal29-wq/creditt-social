Can your parents help you buy without handing over cash?

A family guarantee lets a parent use equity in their own home as extra security for your loan. It can mean a smaller deposit and no LMI, but the guarantor takes on real risk.

Our new guide covers how it works, limited vs full guarantees, the risks for parents and how to get the guarantee released.
