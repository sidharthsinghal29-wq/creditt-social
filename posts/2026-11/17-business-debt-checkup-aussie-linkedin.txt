A lot of the business owners I speak with have one big question after this month's rate rise: is my home loan still working for me?

The RBA lifted the cash rate to 4.85% on 3 November, the fifth increase this year. If you're self-employed, your home loan often does double duty, and in many cases it's also security for business borrowing. So it pays to keep it in good shape.

A few things I'd check:

• When did you last review your home loan? Lenders don't always pass their best pricing to existing customers
• Keep your business financials and bank statements up to date, so you're ready if refinancing makes sense
• Know what your home is securing, and whether that still suits you
• Build in a buffer and check your repayments still work comfortably at today's rates
