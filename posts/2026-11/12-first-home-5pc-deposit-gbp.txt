Buying your first home in Sydney? Under the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme, eligible first home buyers can purchase with a 5% deposit and no lenders mortgage insurance. There are no income caps and no waiting list, and the Sydney price cap is $1.5 million.

Sydney dwelling values are more than 10% below their February peak (Cotality), but five cash rate rises this year have reduced borrowing power for many households.

Before you start looking:
- Get pre-approval so you know your real budget
- Check your price range sits under the scheme cap
- Budget for stamp duty, legal and moving costs on top of your deposit
- Stress-test repayments on a bigger loan
