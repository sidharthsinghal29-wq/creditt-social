First home on your mind? You may only need 5% saved. 🏡

Under the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme, eligible first home buyers can buy with a 5% deposit and no lenders mortgage insurance. No income caps, no waiting list, and the Sydney price cap is $1.5 million.

Before you start looking:
• Get pre-approval first
• Check homes sit under the cap
• Budget for stamp duty and costs on top of 5%
• Stress-test repayments on a bigger loan
