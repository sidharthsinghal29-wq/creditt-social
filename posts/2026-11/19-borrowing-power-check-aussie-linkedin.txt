A question I'm hearing a lot at the moment: "My pre-approval is from earlier in the year. Is it still good?"

It's worth checking. The RBA has lifted the cash rate five times in 2026, by 1.25 percentage points to 4.85%. Lenders assess new borrowers at least 3 percentage points above the loan rate, so the amount you were approved for months ago may not be the amount you can borrow today.

Before you make an offer, I'd suggest:

• Having your borrowing power re-assessed against current rates
• Reducing credit card limits you don't use
• Paying down or closing smaller debts where you can
• Keeping your savings and payslips up to date so you're ready to move
